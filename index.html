🧱 Linking Cube Number Stairs

Build, stack, move, and count real-looking digital cubes!

Drag red or blue cubes from the trays into a stair. You can also click a tray cube, then click a stair. Stack up to 10 cubes in each stair. Drag a cube from one stair to another to move it.
